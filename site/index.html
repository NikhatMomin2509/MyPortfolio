  • PCB Can leach toxic material into soil and water. Our most concerning component.
  • PVC cable Releases harmful fumes if burned. Never burn.
  • Magnet Small but strong; hard to dispose of, recycle properly.
  • Copper strands Sharp ends; handle carefully.

Most valuable: copper wires, neodymium in the speaker driver, engineering plastics.

Cable → Repair, RecycleSpeaker → Reuse, Refurbish, RecyclePCB → Repair, Refurbish, RecycleHousing → Reuse, Refurbish, Recycle

The most valuable material isn't always the easiest to recover, because it's hard to separate from everything around it.

  • Glued housing → screws or snap-fit casing for easy repair.
  • Soldered wires → detachable connectors for easy replacement.
  • Mixed materials → separate modules for easy recycling.

We recommend designing for easy repair because it extends product life and reduces e-waste. After this activity we wouldn't call the device "waste" — its parts can be repaired, reused or recycled.
